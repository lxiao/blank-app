Assumptions & notes

  • GPUs: 16,384; training duration: 54 days.
  • Per-GPU average power: 350 W (PCIe TDP) or 700 W (SXM5 commonly cited).
  • PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness): 1.2 → 20% overhead beyond IT load.
  • Facility energy = IT energy × PUE; equivalence uses 10,500 kWh/home/year (U.S. average).
  • These are transparent, illustrative ranges; actual usage varies with utilization and system design.
